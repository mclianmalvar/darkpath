Dark Path

Defend the last city.

A tear in the sky called the Maw has released the Hollow, monsters of ash and bone. Greywatch is the last city standing, and the next invasion arrives in 30 days.

Tower defenseCity builderDungeon crawler

Survival is the only reward.

There are no unlocks between campaigns and no help after a defeat. Loot and upgrades are tools for staying alive. When a campaign ends, nothing carries over. Your score is the number of people still alive in Greywatch, shown with the names of the heroes who made it.

The countdown

Thirty days to prepare. One night to hold.

No monsters attack during the countdown. The danger is in the dungeons, and in running out of time. Each day you choose: build the city, or send heroes into the dark for power and loot.

Day 1: the surprise siege (first act only) Day 25: scout report Invasion

Current buildAt a new game you choose Act 1's length: the full 30 days, with the scout report on Day 25, or a short 10-day version with it on Day 7.

  1. 1 · Dawn

    Report

    Days left, hero status and expedition results, and what Tomas foresees for the invasion. Every few days a city event is drawn, like refugees at the gate.

  2. 2 · Day

    City and dungeon

    Build, research, pass Decrees, and send a hero party on a 1 to 3 day expedition. Every upgrade and repair costs days off the countdown.

  3. 3 · Evening

    Recover

    Heal wounds, lower Dread and equip new loot. Then the next day begins.

  4. 4 · Countdown ends

    Invasion

    Waves of the Hollow and a boss in real time: three waves in the short Act 1, four in the full one. You command towers, heroes and militia.

  5. 5 · Aftermath

    Rebuild

    Destroyed towers and walls become ruins. Clear them, count losses, and a new countdown begins.

!

Day 1: the horns sound early

The game opens on an ordinary morning. Before you have built anything, the Hollow attack. About half of new players should hold the walls. Win or lose, the story goes on, but a loss costs you the Market Ring.

Two fronts

Command the walls. Crawl the dark.

Heroes fight in both places with the same gear, so a well-equipped dungeon party is a stronger garrison when the siege comes.

Invasions · real time

On the walls

  • RTS command with active pause. Select heroes, militia squads and the Warden, then right-click to move or attack. Hold Shift to queue orders one after another.
  • Formations. Hold the right mouse button and drag a line, as in Total War. A long line spreads your troops wide, a short one stacks them in ranks, and it works along the top of a wall too.
  • Towers that grow in three steps. Archer Post to Archer Tower to Longbow Nest, Ballista to Heavy Ballista to Piercer, Frost Bell to Rime Bell to Glacier.
  • Tall walls. Build towers on top of the walls for extra range, and send units up the wall stairs to fight from the walkway. The North wall is two tiles thick, so units can walk along it, pass each other and cross over the gates. The walls are not a safe place to stand, though: Gloomwings hunt the defenders on them, a Brute pounding the wall shakes them, and if the wall breaks they fall.
  • Battle honors and veterans. After every battle the report names its MVP. Squads that survive become Blooded, then Veteran, and fight harder for it.
  • Bait Carts. A cart of blood and offal draws nearby Hollow to it before anything else. Build one where your towers can reach them.
  • Vael's Whisper. Once per invasion, Vael turns one of the Hollow against its own kind for twenty seconds.
  • Habyr's Hatred. Press G and for 12 seconds Habyr hits harder with every kill, and each kill heals him a little.
  • A last stand. A downed hero can spend their last seconds on their feet, unbreakable, and their example steels everyone else for the rest of the battle.
  • Post them before the horn. During the countdown your troops stand in the city and take the same orders. Wherever they stand when the last day ends is where the battle begins.
  • A crewed Catapult. Put a squad on it, click a target area, and spend the Stone Shot you stockpiled. Trebuchets and cannons come later.
  • Time of day. The Hollow arrive at dawn, noon, dusk or night. At night, tower and archer range drops by 20%.
  • Evacuate the Market Ring. Press B and its citizens walk to the Keep, with the Hollow hunting them on the roads.

Expeditions · turn based

In the dungeons

  • A party in ranks. Each skill can only be used from certain ranks and hits certain enemy ranks. In Act 1 the party is Bram, Sera and Habyr; the full game allows four.
  • Habyr. On the first expedition the party finds him fighting alone in the Old Mines. Every blow he lands adds to his Hatred, and with it his damage, until the fight ends.
  • Timed strikes and parries. Press Space as the marker crosses the zone for bonus damage or a perfect block.
  • Dread and Afflictions. Fear builds until a hero turns Panicked, Reckless or Hopeless.
  • Levels and loot. Heroes level up and bring home weapons, chest armor and trinkets.
  • Auto-timing resolves strikes and parries for you at most of the bonus, on any difficulty.
  • Relics from the Root. The last gallery of the Old Mines holds pieces of the Watch Horn. Each piece strengthens the Warden, and the whole horn turns Overclock into a Rallying Horn.

Greywatch

One city that grows across the whole campaign.

The whole campaign is played on one map of four rings. New districts, walls and invasion lanes open as you reclaim ground. Click a ring to see what it holds.

The torch meter

The darker the room, the better the loot.

Your party carries a torch that burns down as it explores. You can push on into the dark on purpose. It is more dangerous, and it pays better.

Story and world

The kingdom of Veyra fell in a year.

Veyra called it peace: gambling halls, crime and cruelty. Then a tear opened in the sky, the Hollow poured out, and within a year every city fell except Greywatch, a fortress on a sea cliff above old mines and crypts.

Its leader saw it coming. A man from another world had woken in his place: he named the Maw, trained the city and forged its weapons, and found a great tree with a doorway the Hollow cannot pass. He was never meant to lead, so he chose Rook Varrel, you, as Warden of Greywatch, and stays at your side as your advisor. The tone is dark and grim, with warmth from the people around you: a story about survival, sacrifice and what power does to a person.

The cast

Any one of them can die for good.

Story heroes have personal quests and portrait dialogue. Random recruits hired at the Inn fill out the roster with generated names and quirks. Every hero matters in the city, in the dungeons and on the walls.

The Memorial Wall

Every death is permanent. Every name is carved.

  • Autosave only, on every difficulty.One save per campaign, written at each Dawn. No reloads.
  • Fallen heroes can still be saved, briefly.In an invasion, any ally has 15 seconds to reach a downed hero.
  • Every name is kept.The fallen are carved into the Memorial Wall. In the full game, each one will also leave a small lasting bonus.
  • If the Warden falls, Greywatch falls.The game warns you at 30% health and offers a one-click retreat to the Keep.
