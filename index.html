Dark Path

Defend the last city.

A tear in the sky called the Maw has released the Hollow, monsters of ash and bone. Greywatch is the last city standing, and the next invasion arrives in 30 days.

Tower defenseCity builderDungeon crawler

Survival is the only reward.

There are no unlocks between campaigns and no help after a defeat. Loot and upgrades are tools for staying alive. When a campaign ends, nothing carries over but its record. Your score is the number of people still alive in Greywatch, shown with the names of the heroes who made it.

The countdown

Thirty days to prepare. One night to hold.

No monsters attack during the countdown. The danger is in the dungeons, and in running out of time. Each day you choose: build the city, or send heroes into the dark for power and loot.

Day 1: the surprise siege (first act only) Day 25: scout report Invasion

Current buildAt a new game you choose the mode, the difficulty and the length: Full, with Act 1's 30 days and the scout report on Day 25, or Short, with a 10-day Act 1 and the scout report on Day 7. In Endless, every countdown after the fourth act is shorter than the last.

  1. 1 · Dawn

    Report

    Days left, hero status and expedition results, and what Tomas foresees for the invasion. Every few days a city event is drawn, like refugees at the gate.

  2. 2 · Day

    City and dungeon

    Build, research, pass Decrees, and send a hero party on a 1 to 3 day expedition. Every upgrade and repair costs days off the countdown.

  3. 3 · Evening

    Recover

    Heal wounds, lower Dread and equip new loot. Then the next day begins.

  4. 4 · Countdown ends

    Invasion

    Waves of the Hollow and a boss in real time: three waves in the short Act 1, four in the full one. You command towers, heroes and militia.

  5. 5 · Aftermath

    Rebuild

    Destroyed towers and walls become ruins. Clear them, count losses, and a new countdown begins.

!

Day 1: the horns sound early

The game opens on an ordinary morning. Before you have built anything, the Hollow attack. About half of new players should hold the walls. Win or lose, the story goes on, but a loss costs you the Market Ring.

Two fronts

Command the walls. Crawl the dark.

Heroes fight in both places with the same gear, so a well-equipped dungeon party is a stronger garrison when the siege comes.

Invasions · real time

On the walls

  • RTS command with active pause. Select heroes, militia squads and the Warden, then right-click to move or attack. Hold Shift to queue orders one after another.
  • Formations. Hold the right mouse button and drag a line, as in Total War. A long line spreads your troops wide, a short one stacks them in ranks, and it works along the top of a wall too.
  • Nine defenses, three steps each, a choice at the last. The Archer Post becomes a Longbow Nest or a Crossbow Hall, the Ballista a Piercer or a Harpoon, the Frost Bell a Glacier or a Chill Aura, the Flame Pit an Oil Trench or an Inferno, and so on for the Arcane Spire, Spike Trap, Watchtower, Chapel and the walls.
  • Synergies. A frozen Hollow shatters under a heavy shot. Fire sets an Oil Trench ablaze, and the next one with it. A Ballista bolt on a Hollow a Watchtower marks is a critical hit.
  • Tall walls. Build towers on top of the walls for extra range, and send units up the wall stairs to fight from the walkway. The North wall is two tiles thick, so units can walk along it, pass each other and cross over the gates. The walls are not a safe place to stand, though: Gloomwings hunt the defenders on them, a Brute pounding the wall shakes them, and if the wall breaks they fall.
  • Battle honors and veterans. After every battle the report names its MVP. Squads that survive become Blooded, then Veteran, and fight harder for it.
  • Bait Carts. A cart of blood and offal draws nearby Hollow to it before anything else. Build one where your towers can reach them.
  • Vael's Whisper. Once per invasion, Vael turns one of the Hollow against its own kind for twenty seconds.
  • Habyr's Hatred. Press G and for 12 seconds Habyr hits harder with every kill, and each kill heals him a little.
  • Isolde's Frostfire and Ilse's Vigil. Press Q and click: every Hollow there freezes for two seconds, then the ground bursts into witchfire. Press E and Mother Ilse heals everyone around her by a third and lifts a downed hero back to their feet.
  • Recruits fight by their class. Warriors hold back two Hollow at once, clerics heal, rangers and wizards shoot, bards sing the defenders on, doctors get the downed up faster.
  • A last stand. A downed hero can spend their last seconds on their feet, unbreakable, and their example steels everyone else for the rest of the battle.
  • Post them before the horn. During the countdown your troops stand in the city and take the same orders. Wherever they stand when the last day ends is where the battle begins.
  • Crewed siege engines with firing arcs. Put a squad on the Catapult, the Trebuchet or the Cannon and it fires on its own, but only into its firing arc: a wedge in front of it, shown in orange when you select it, as in Total War. Click to turn it; it swings round at its own pace, the Trebuchet slowest. It spends the ammo you stockpiled: Stone Shot or Fire Pots, Iron Shot or Grapeshot (T switches it). A Catapult's or Trebuchet's shot bursts in a cone that sprays on past where it lands. The Cannon is loud: Gloomwings come for its crew.
  • A larger city. The Godot source expands Greywatch to 64×64 tiles, with two new wards behind extended curtain walls. Defenders and the Hollow have richer walking, idle and attack motion.
  • The Phalanx. Select Spearmen, press P and click where they should face. In the Godot source, the formation uses their current steel-rimmed wooden shields. They lock shields in ranks and hold there: their spears reach over the front rank, they hit harder and shrug off half of every blow from the front, but they strike only the Hollow in front of them.
  • Time of day, weather and Omens. The Hollow arrive at dawn, noon, dusk or night, in clear skies, rain, fog, snow or wind, and from the second invasion under an Omen such as a Blood Moon. The scout report tells you, so the last days of the countdown are for adapting.
  • The Hollow adapt. If one kind of tower deals more than 40% of the damage in an invasion, the next host resists it.
  • Evacuate the Market Ring. Press B and its citizens walk to the Keep, with the Hollow hunting them on the roads. Build Shelters there to hold them, or the crowding costs morale and may bring sickness.

Expeditions · turn based

In the dungeons

  • A party of up to four, in ranks. Each skill can only be used from certain ranks and hits certain enemy ranks.
  • Four regions, one more each act. The Old Mines, the Drowned Crypts, the Ashen Warrens and the Root of the Maw, each deeper and deadlier than the last, and each paying better.
  • Short, Medium or Long runs. A Short run takes a day. Medium and Long runs take two and three, end at the region's boss, and a Long run makes camp halfway: spend its points on bandages, a watch, sharpened blades, stories or a prayer.
  • Heroes found below. Habyr fights alone in the Old Mines, and Isolde, a hedge witch, waits in the Drowned Crypts. Mother Ilse comes home with the farmers when the Farm Ring is retaken.
  • Recruits from the Inn. Hire warriors, clerics, rangers, bards, doctors and wizards, each with a generated name and face, a quirk or two, and a class's own skills. Up to eight heroes on the roster.
  • Timed strikes and parries. Press Space as the marker crosses the zone for bonus damage or a perfect block.
  • Dread and Afflictions. Fear builds until a hero turns Panicked, Reckless or Hopeless.
  • Hollow Taint. The dark and the elites' blows corrupt a hero: stronger at first, then they may strike at friends, and at 100 they turn Hollow. Mother Ilse or a Sanctum can cleanse it.
  • Bonds. Heroes who fight together grow close, hit harder side by side, and a Sworn pair shares a pair skill.
  • Levels and loot. Heroes level up and bring home weapons, chest armor and trinkets.
  • Auto-timing resolves strikes and parries for you at most of the bonus, on any difficulty.
  • Relics. The last gallery of the Old Mines holds pieces of the Watch Horn, which strengthens the Warden. Each region's boss drops a Relic of its own, a trinket like no other.
  • The Heart of the Maw. Destroy it at the bottom of the Root, and the Unmade King comes to the last invasion weaker, with fewer of his dead.

The torch meter

The darker the room, the better the loot.

Your party carries a torch that burns down as it explores. You can push on into the dark on purpose. It is more dangerous, and it pays better. The Godot source gives heroes detailed 128×128 artwork, eight-frame walks, idle breathing and attack/recovery motion. Dungeon enemies also animate, and heroes turn as they explore.

The Hollow

Pale ash and bone, glowing at the cracks.

Every invasion is stronger than the last, and the Hollow adapt to the defenses you lean on. In the game, the Bestiary fills in a page as you kill each kind of monster: what it is, its numbers at 10 kills, its weakness at 25, and at 50 you have studied it and hit it harder. Try a taste of it here: hunt a monster to learn what it is, then its type, then how to beat it.

Story and world

The kingdom of Veyra fell in a year.

Veyra called it peace: gambling halls, crime and cruelty. Then a tear opened in the sky, the Hollow poured out, and within a year every city fell except Greywatch, a fortress on a sea cliff above old mines and crypts.

Its leader saw it coming. A man from another world had woken in his place: he named the Maw, trained the city and forged its weapons, and found a great tree with a doorway the Hollow cannot pass. He was never meant to lead, so he chose Rook Varrel, you, as Warden of Greywatch, and stays at your side as your advisor. The tone is dark and grim, with warmth from the people around you: a story about survival, sacrifice and what power does to a person.

The cast

Any one of them can die for good.

Story heroes have personal quests and portrait dialogue. Random recruits hired at the Inn fill out the roster with generated names and quirks. Every hero matters in the city, in the dungeons and on the walls.

The Memorial Wall

Every death is permanent. Every name is carved.

  • Autosave only, on every difficulty.One save per campaign, written at each Dawn. No reloads.
  • Fallen heroes can still be saved, briefly.In an invasion, any ally has 15 seconds to reach a downed hero.
  • Every name is kept.The fallen are carved into the Memorial Wall, and each leaves something behind: a fallen recruit raises the floor under the city's morale, a fallen story hero leaves a gift of their own, such as Sera's farther-reaching Archer Posts.
  • If the Warden falls, Greywatch falls.The game warns you at 30% health and offers a one-click retreat to the Keep.

When a hero dies, every bonded partner grieves: Dread, and less damage for five days. Habyr turns it to fury.
