How to play it

  • Unzip it anywhere. There is no installer: the DarkPath folder holds the game and a short readme.
  • Run DarkPath.exe. The game opens in a maximized window; Esc opens the menu, and Settings has the controls, the interface size, music and the colorblind-friendly health bars.
  • If Windows warns you. The game is not signed yet, so Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Click More info, then Run anyway.
  • Saves. Autosave only, one campaign at a time, kept in %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Dark Path.

What's in this build

  • The Prologue: the surprise siege of Greywatch, with both of its outcomes.
  • Four acts, chosen at a new game as a Full campaign (countdowns of 30, 30, 25 and 20 days) or a Short one (10, 10, 9 and 8), with twenty city events and a story beat in each act.
  • The city: seven buildings with the Inn, workers, militia squads, the Farm Ring to retake in Act 2, and five Decrees that lead to the Tyrant or the Guardian ending.
  • Nine defenses and the Bait Cart, each defense with a choice of two branches at its last upgrade, three synergies between them, and three siege engines (the Catapult, the Trebuchet and the Cannon) with four kinds of ammo.
  • Four dungeon regions under the Root, from the Old Mines to the Root of the Maw, in Short, Medium or Long runs, each with its boss and a Relic. Bram and Sera, Habyr in the Old Mines, Isolde in the Drowned Crypts, Mother Ilse with the Farm Ring, and recruits hired at the Inn.
  • Four invasions: Gravemaw, the Mother of Swarms, the Pale Twins and the Unmade King, with Spitters, Burrowers, Shriekers and the siege beasts from the second on. Heroes, militia, the Catapult and Vael's Whisper hold the walls.
  • Weather and Omens for every invasion, Shelters for the evacuated, and invasions that resist the defense you lean on most.
  • The Bestiary, hero bonds and Grief, Hollow Taint, and the legacies of the fallen on the Memorial Wall.
  • Hand-drawn 32×32 art, music and ambience, rebindable keys.
  • First-time hints for the newer mechanics, and a battle log after every battle.

Endless mode and the Challenges are planned. See the roadmap.

Feedback

This is a playtest, so expect rough edges. Bugs, crashes and how your campaign went are all welcome on GitHub issues. News about new builds goes in the devlog.

Battle logs help most. After every battle the game saves a short text report in %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Dark Path\battle_logs, and the battle summary has a button that opens the folder. Attach the files to your issue: they say how each wave went, who fought, and what it cost.
