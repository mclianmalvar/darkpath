Description

Rule the last walled city of a fallen kingdom. You have 30 days before each invasion: build your defenses, send heroes into the dungeons for power and loot, then hold the walls.

A tear in the sky called the Maw has released the Hollow, monsters of ash and bone. Greywatch is the last city standing, and the next invasion arrives in 30 days. As its Warden, you spend each day building towers, running the economy and sending hero parties into dangerous dungeons, where turn-based battles level them up and win them rare equipment. When the countdown ends, the Hollow attack, and you command towers, heroes and militia in a real-time siege. Every invasion is stronger than the last, and every harsh law you pass to survive changes what kind of ruler you become.

Dark Path is inspired by games about defending a city from waves of monsters. Its invasion controls draw on Total War: Warhammer III and its dungeon battles on Darkest Dungeon. The world, characters and story are original.

Key features

The Godot source now uses 128×128 sprite textures, with new detailed defender artwork featuring layered armor, sculpted faces and distinct weapons. Enemies and structures have finer material detail. This source art update is not yet in the downloadable Windows playtest build.

  • Godot source: a 64×64 Greywatch with two new wards, detailed city and defense sprites, and expanded character animation cycles. Earlier map and art remain in the playtest download.
  • Prepare, then survive. A 30-day countdown makes every day a choice: build the city, or send heroes into the dungeons. Every upgrade and repair costs days of it, and your troops can be posted on the walls before the horn.
  • Two ways to fight. Turn-based dungeon battles with timed strikes and parries, and real-time command of heroes and militia during invasions, with drag-to-set formations and queued orders.
  • Defenses that grow your way. Nine defenses, each with a choice of two branches at its last upgrade, synergies between them, and three crewed siege engines firing the ammo you stockpiled.
  • Every battle remembered. Each siege names its MVP, squads that survive become Veterans, and a hero can choose a last stand that the Memorial Wall keeps.
  • Loot that defends the city. Gear and Relics found in four dungeon regions make heroes stronger when the siege comes, and destroying the Heart of the Maw weakens the final boss.
  • A roster that can die. Story heroes found in the dark, and recruits of six classes hired at the Inn, each with quirks of their own.
  • Heroes who grow close. Heroes who fight together bond, hit harder side by side and share a pair skill; when one dies, the others grieve. The dark leaves Hollow Taint on them, and the fallen leave a legacy on the Memorial Wall.
  • Decrees. Harsh laws grant great power at a cost: five of them, from Conscription to Burn the Outskirts. Every one you pass is remembered, and it decides whether you save the city as a Tyrant or as its Guardian.
  • Adaptive invasions. The Hollow resist the defenses you relied on most, and every invasion comes with its own weather and, later, an Omen.
  • Endless and the Challenges. After Invasion IV, Endless keeps the Hollow coming, each countdown shorter and each host larger, ranked by days survived. No Walls and No Dungeons take away a pillar of the city.
  • Survival is the only reward. Permanent death on every difficulty, autosave only, and a score counted in citizens still alive.

History

Dark Path is a solo project by Mc Lian M. Malvar, built at a hobby pace in Godot 4. The game design document reached version 1.0 and was frozen on October 19, 2026, after twenty revisions. Act 1 now runs as a browser MVP, covering the Prologue, a 10-day countdown, the Old Mines and Invasion I. The official website launched on October 20, 2026. The same day, the Godot 4.3 version of Act 1 went up as a free Windows playtest build, with hand-drawn animated sprites, music, a third hero (Habyr), the first Decrees and the full 30-day countdown. On October 21 the build grew to the whole campaign: four acts and four invasions, from Gravemaw to the Unmade King, with both endings.
