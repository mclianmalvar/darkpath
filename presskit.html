Description

Rule the last walled city of a fallen kingdom. You have 30 days before each invasion: build your defenses, send heroes into the dungeons for power and loot, then hold the walls.

A tear in the sky called the Maw has released the Hollow, monsters of ash and bone. Greywatch is the last city standing, and the next invasion arrives in 30 days. As its Warden, you spend each day building towers, running the economy and sending hero parties into dangerous dungeons, where turn-based battles level them up and win them rare equipment. When the countdown ends, the Hollow attack, and you command towers, heroes and militia in a real-time siege. Every invasion is stronger than the last, and every harsh law you pass to survive changes what kind of ruler you become.

Dark Path is inspired by games about defending a city from waves of monsters. Its invasion controls draw on Total War: Warhammer III and its dungeon battles on Darkest Dungeon. The world, characters and story are original.

Key features

  • Prepare, then survive. A 30-day countdown makes every day a choice: build the city, or send heroes into the dungeons.
  • Two ways to fight. Turn-based dungeon battles with timed strikes and parries, and real-time command of heroes and militia during invasions, with drag-to-set formations and queued orders.
  • Loot that defends the city. Gear and Relics found in the dungeons make heroes stronger when the siege comes.
  • Decrees. Harsh laws grant great power at a cost to morale, and lead to a Tyrant or a Guardian ending.
  • Adaptive invasions. The Hollow change to counter the defenses you relied on most.
  • Survival is the only reward. Permanent death on every difficulty, autosave only, and a score counted in citizens still alive.

History

Dark Path is a solo project by Mc Lian M. Malvar, built at a hobby pace in Godot 4. The game design document reached version 1.0 and was frozen on October 19, 2026, after twenty revisions. Act 1 now runs as a browser MVP, covering the Prologue, a 10-day countdown, the Old Mines and Invasion I. The Godot 4.3 version is in progress. The official website launched on October 20, 2026.
